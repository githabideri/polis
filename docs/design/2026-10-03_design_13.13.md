
## Resolution (2026-10-04, sequencer implemented)

Issue 13.13 is resolved by the build sequencer (`r2/sequencer.py`,
contract test T6, 5/5):

- **The dead-end is a provable geometry fact, not a bot bug.**
  `standable()` (open + supported + not reserved) and the
  stand-greedy peel reproduce the recorded failure exactly (T6b:
  stuck at tier 2+, zero standable candidates - the run-14..17
  signature) and prove no in-layer reorder can save a closed
  cluster (last-cell theorem, doc item 4 of the module).
- **Scaffold policy:** the platform is the minimal set of open
  interior cells whose stand-above is needed (stands-up-stands,
  doc item 3): 24 blocks for the 5x5 hut, torn down in reverse
  after the last layer.
- **The plan carries its own openings.** `builds/hut.json`
  declares three architectural openings (east window (4,2), north
  window (3,3), skylight (2,4)); the sequencer never fills them and
  uses them as permanent stands, so the deterministic peel closes
  the last cell of every closed 3x3 cluster. **Result: the 5x5
  completes with zero notes, fully deterministic, resume-safe.**
  Openings must be supported from below - never two stacked
  (the upper floats; this bit us once during the design search).

  13.13-recorded site state + the run-17 partial world). Live
